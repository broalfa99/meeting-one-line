## The line

> We gathered twelve people for one hour to learn that the only blocker was this meeting.

87 characters — the line kills the meeting outright: after it, no one can justify holding the meeting at all, because the meeting itself was the only thing blocking the work. Twelve people, one hour, and the payoff is that the blocker was the gathering — the most relatable office experience there is. One sentence, no setup, the sting lands on the last three words.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and checks the competition gate itself first: UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, a single line, not only whitespace.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
